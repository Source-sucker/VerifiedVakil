STANDARD RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT (MODEL TENANCY ACT COMPLIANT)

This Agreement is made on this 1st day of October 2026, by and between:
LANDLORD: Mrs. S. Sundaram, residing at Jayanagar, Bangalore;
AND
TENANT: Ms. Neha Kapoor, residing at Bangalore.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. TENANCY PERIOD:
The term of this agreement shall be for a period of 11 months commencing from 1st October 2026. Any renewal of this agreement shall be on mutually agreed terms.

2. MONTHLY RENT:
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 30,000/- on or before the 5th day of every calendar month. Delayed payments shall attract simple interest at 8% per annum as per statutory norms.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Tenant has deposited an advance amount of Rs. 60,000/-, strictly equivalent to 2 months' rent as deposit. The Landlord agrees to refund the security deposit within 3 days of handing over vacant possession of the premises after adjusting legitimate electricity dues.

4. NOTICE PERIOD FOR TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this tenancy by giving 30 days notice in writing to the other party, or payment of one month's rent in lieu thereof.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD:
The parties agree to an initial lock-in duration of 3 months. After 3 months, either party may terminate following the standard 30-day notice period without penalty.

6. INSPECTION AND RIGHT TO ENTER:
The Landlord or property manager may inspect the premises only after serving at least 24 hours prior written notice to the Tenant. Such entry shall occur only between 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM on working days.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS:
Structural repairs, external painting, and main water/electrical supply lines shall be borne by the Landlord. Day-to-day minor maintenance (such as bulb replacements and minor tap washer fixes) shall be borne by the Tenant.

8. PEACEFUL ENJOYMENT:
The Tenant shall have peaceful possession and quiet enjoyment of the premises during the subsistence of this agreement without unreasonable interference.

9. SUBLETTING RESTRICTION:
The Tenant shall not sublet or assign this agreement to any third party without prior written consent from the Landlord.

10. RENT ESCALATION:
In the event of mutual renewal for a subsequent year, the rent shall increase by a maximum escalation of 5% per annum.
