RESIDENTIAL LEAVE AND LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on this 1st day of October 2026, by and between:
LICENSOR: Mr. R. K. Sharma, residing at Indiranagar, Bangalore (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensor");
AND
LICENSEE: Mr. Aditya Verma, employed at Tech Park, Bangalore (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. DURATION AND TERM:
The term of this license shall be for a period of 11 months commencing from 1st October 2026 to 31st August 2027.

2. MONTHLY RENT AND CHARGES:
The Licensee shall pay a monthly license fee of Rs. 35,000/- (Rupees Thirty Five Thousand only) per month, payable in advance on or before the 1st day of each English calendar month. Late payments shall attract a penal fine of Rs. 1,000 per day of delay.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable Security Deposit of Rs. 3,50,000/- (Rupees Three Lakhs Fifty Thousand only), equivalent to 10 months rent as deposit, to the Licensor. 

4. TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIOD:
The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time with 0 days notice without assigning any reason whatsoever. If the Licensee desires to vacate, the Licensee must provide 3 months notice in writing.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD AND FORFEITURE:
There shall be a strict lock-in period of 11 months. If the Licensee vacates the premises before the expiry of the lock-in duration, the entire security deposit shall stand forfeited by the Licensor as liquidated damages.

6. LANDLORD ENTRY AND INSPECTION RIGHTS:
The Licensor or his authorized agents shall have the unconditional right to enter and inspect the premises at any time without notice, day or night, to check the condition of the flat.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS RESPONSIBILITY:
All repairs, whether minor day-to-day repairs or structural repairs including wall seepage, roof leakage, major pipeline replacement, and electrical wiring shall be borne exclusively by the Licensee at his own cost.

8. PAINTING DEDUCTION ON EXIT:
Upon termination or vacating the premises, the Licensor shall deduct a non-negotiable sum equivalent to one full month's rent (Rs. 35,000/-) from the security deposit towards painting charges, irrespective of actual wall condition.

9. SUBLETTING AND GUEST RESTRICTIONS:
The Licensee shall not sublet or part with possession of the premises. Overnight guests or visitors after 9:00 PM are strictly prohibited without prior written consent.

10. ANNUAL RENT ESCALATION:
Upon any extension or renewal of this license, the monthly rent shall increase by an enhancement of 15% per annum.
